# Hi, I'm Alessio 👋

I am a Master's student in Artificial Intelligence, and my research interests and goals lie at the intersection of AI and Neuroscience. What drives me is a deep curiosity to understand how the human brain works and an ambition to develop brain-based technologies. 

My primary focus is **brain decoding**, which involves interpreting what a person is perceiving, thinking, or dreaming directly from their brain activity, with the ultimate goal of leveraging these technologies to build tools that help patients recover lost functions and overcome physical and communicative barriers.
